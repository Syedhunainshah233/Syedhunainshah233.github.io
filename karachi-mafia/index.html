Karachi Mafia

Open-world action game for Android.

About the game

Karachi Mafia is a lightweight open-world game set in a city inspired by Karachi. Walk the streets, jump into any car, meet the people of the city, and finish short missions to earn coins. Characters talk in Roman Urdu.

  • Open city with roads, houses and shops
  • Drive cars and explore freely
  • Short missions with coin rewards
  • Simple graphics made to run smoothly on low-end phones
  • Optional sign-in with Google

The game is free to play and shows ads. All characters, events and violence in the game are fictional.
