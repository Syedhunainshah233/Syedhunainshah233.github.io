Privacy Policy

Karachi Mafia · Effective 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Karachi Mafia (package com.syedtechdevelopers.karachimafia) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by SyedTechdevelopers ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

Google Sign-In (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID from Google. We use these only to show your profile in the game and to link your game progress to your account. We never receive your Google password.

Game data

Your progress (coins, unlocked cars and weapons, completed missions and settings) is stored on your device.

Advertising data

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the game crashes, Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us so we can fix problems.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress
  • To let you sign in with Google
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch
  • To fix bugs and improve the game

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the game: Google (Sign-In, AdMob and Google Play services). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Ad personalization and consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change this choice later from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. You can sign out of Google at any time from the game's settings. To ask us to delete any data linked to your account, email syedhunainshah120@gmail.com with the subject "Karachi Mafia data deletion". We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Data sent between the game and Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

Karachi Mafia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
