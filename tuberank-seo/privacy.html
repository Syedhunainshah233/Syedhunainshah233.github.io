Privacy Policy

TubeRank SEO · Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app TubeRank SEO (package com.syedtechdevelopers.tuberankseo) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by SyedTechdevelopers ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

No account needed

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not run our own servers that store your data.

Keywords you type

When you tap Generate, the keyword or topic you typed is sent over an encrypted connection to Google's public search-suggestion service to fetch popular related searches. Only the keyword and the language you chose are sent. Please do not type personal information (such as names, phone numbers or addresses) as a keyword.

History and settings on your device

Your generated results, history and settings (such as language) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

Advertising data

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us so we can fix problems.

2. How we use information

  • To generate titles, descriptions, tags and hashtags for your keyword
  • To save your history on your device
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch
  • To fix bugs and improve the app

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app: Google (search suggestions, AdMob and Google Play services). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Ad personalization and consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change this choice later from the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

History stays on your device until you delete it from the app (Settings → Clear history), clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Because we do not keep your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; for any privacy question, email syedhunainshah120@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security
