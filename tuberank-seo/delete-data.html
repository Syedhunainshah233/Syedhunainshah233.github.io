Delete your data

TubeRank SEO by SyedTechdevelopers

TubeRank SEO has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run servers that store your data. Your history and settings are saved only on your phone. You can delete them yourself, or ask us for help.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open TubeRank SEO.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom.
  3. Tap Clear history, then Delete.

To remove everything (history, settings, free-generation counter and PRO timer), go to your phone's Settings → Apps → TubeRank SEO → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Option 2: Email us

Send an email to syedhunainshah120@gmail.com with the subject "TubeRank SEO data deletion request". We reply within 30 days and explain how to remove any data linked to the app.

What is deleted

  • Generated results and history saved on your phone
  • App settings (language, region, platform)
  • Free-generation count and PRO timer

What is kept, and for how long

  • We keep nothing: no account, name, email or history is stored on our side.
  • Keywords you type are sent to Google only to fetch search suggestions and are not stored by us.
  • Advertising data (such as your advertising ID) is handled by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy and its retention periods. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
