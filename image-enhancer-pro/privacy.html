Privacy Policy

Image Enhancer Pro – AI 4K · Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Image Enhancer Pro – AI 4K (package com.syedtechdevelopers.imageenhancerpro) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by SyedTechdevelopers ("we", "us").

1. Your photos stay on your phone

When you pick a photo and tap Enhance, the AI runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see them. Face detection also runs on your device (Google ML Kit, offline). Enhanced photos are saved only where you choose (your gallery) or shared only when you tap Share.

The app uses Android's photo picker, so it can only open the photos you select. It does not read the rest of your gallery.

2. Information we collect

No account needed

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not run our own servers that store your data.

Usage limits on your device

The number of free enhances used and the unlock timers are saved only on your device.

Advertising data

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the app crashes, Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us so we can fix problems.

3. How we use information

  • To enhance the photos you choose, on your device
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch to enhance photos or unlock 1 hour of unlimited enhancing
  • To fix bugs and improve the app

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app: Google (AdMob and Google Play services). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Ad personalization and consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change this choice later from the app's info screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep no data on our servers. Your limits and timers stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. For any privacy question, email syedhunainshah120@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Ad requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Your photos are processed offline on your device.

8. Children

Image Enhancer Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Open-source AI models

The app uses the open-source models Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3-Clause) and GFPGAN (Apache-2.0).
